# Travel & Expense Policy

## Booking travel
All business travel must be booked through the company travel portal at least **14 days** in advance.
Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed above 6 hours.

## Daily allowances
| Expense      | Limit per day |
|--------------|---------------|
| Meals        | €50           |
| Hotel (EU)   | €180          |
| Hotel (US)   | $250          |
| Local transit| €30           |

## Submitting expenses
Submit receipts in the expense system within **30 days** of the trip. Expenses without a receipt above
€25 are not reimbursed. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## Non-reimbursable items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, fines, and upgrades not covered above.
