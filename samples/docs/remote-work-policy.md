# Remote Work Policy

_Last updated: 2026-01-15 · Owner: People Operations_

## Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 3-month probation period may work remotely.
Contractors need written approval from their engagement manager.

## How much remote work is allowed
- Employees may work remotely **up to three days per week**.
- Teams must agree on at least one shared office day ("anchor day") per week.
- Fully remote arrangements require VP approval and are reviewed every 12 months.

## Core hours
Everyone must be reachable on chat and video between **10:00 and 15:00** in their local time zone.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop, monitor and headset. A one-off home-office stipend of **€400** can be
claimed through the expense system within 60 days of starting remote work.

## Security
- Always connect through the corporate VPN.
- Do not work from public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your device.
