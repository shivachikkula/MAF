# Engineering Onboarding Guide

## Week 1
1. Collect your laptop from IT and enroll it in device management.
2. Request access to GitHub, the cloud console, and the on-call tool via the access portal.
3. Pair with your onboarding buddy on a "good first issue".

## Development workflow
- Every change goes through a pull request with at least **one approving review**.
- CI must be green before merging; flaky tests are fixed, never skipped.
- Deploys to production happen automatically after merge to `main`, Monday to Thursday.

## On-call
New engineers join the on-call rotation after **8 weeks**. Shifts are one week long and
are compensated with one extra day off per shift.
